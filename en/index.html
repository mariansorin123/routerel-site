The problem

It's not you. It's the way people study.

If you've already tried to study for the CCNA, you probably know the feeling.

Hundreds of pages, no clarity

You read chapter after chapter and still don't know what really matters for the exam.

You forget what you read

What you don't review fades. A week later you're not sure about things you thought you understood.

You don't know if you're ready

You book the exam on a hunch. Or you postpone it. Again.

Every attempt costs money

The CCNA exam (200-301) costs around USD 300, according to Cisco's listed price. Taking it twice hurts.

The fix isn't reading more. It's practising smarter.

How it works

The Routerel method: practise, understand, repeat, test yourself.

Four simple steps, a little every day. Each one fixes one of the problems above.

  2. 1

    Practise a little, every day

    Sessions of 10 or 20 questions, with mixed topics. Do them on a break, on the bus or in the evening.

  3. 2

    Understand on the spot

    After every answer you see the explanation and why the other options are wrong. You learn the why, not just the what.

  4. 3

    Mistakes come back until you know them

    What you get wrong comes back the next day, then in a day, three days, a week. What you know comes back less and less often, so you don't forget it.

  5. 4

    Test yourself like in the exam

    A simulation with 100 questions and 120 minutes, from CCNA 1, 2 and 3. See your score per part and per topic, and know what's left to work on.

Who it's for

Who Routerel is for (and who it isn't for)

It's for you if

  • You're doing CCNA 1, 2 or 3 and want the material to stick, not just to read it.
  • You're preparing for the CCNA exam and want to know when you're ready.
  • You're short on time and want to study in short breaks, on your phone.
  • You want to practise in the exam's English, with Romanian one tap away.

It's not for you if

  • You want to memorise answers without understanding them. Here you learn the why.
  • You need theory from scratch, chapter by chapter. Routerel is for practising and locking in what you learn.
  • You want hands-on configuration practice on real devices. Routerel doesn't replace a lab.
  • You have an iPhone. For now, Routerel is Android only.

FAQ

Questions before you start

Can I try it for free?

Yes: the first 7 days of your subscription are free, with full access, if you haven't had a Routerel subscription before. And if you just want a quick look first, you can answer a few questions without starting the trial.

How do I cancel? Do I really pay nothing during the 7 days?

You pay nothing during the trial. If you cancel before it ends, you pay nothing at all. You cancel in Google Play: Play Store → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Routerel → Cancel. After cancelling, you keep access until the end of the paid period.

I'm busy. Does a few minutes a day really work?

Yes, that's what it's built for. A session is 10 questions (or 20, if you like) and takes a few minutes. You choose a daily goal (10, 20, 30 or 50 questions) and the app reminds you at the time you pick. Coming back every day matters more than long sessions.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The questions are stored on your phone, and answers given offline sync on their own when you're back online. You need internet to create your account, to subscribe (that's when the questions download) and, now and then, to check your subscription.

Which languages are the questions in?

English or Romanian, your choice. The EN / RO button at the top right switches the whole app, any time. Technical terms stay in English, like in the exam.

Are these the real exam questions?

No. We write them ourselves, as exam-style exercises on the CCNA 1, 2 and 3 topics, each with an explanation. We don't copy questions from exams, books or other sources. To pass, you need to understand, and that's exactly what you practise here.

Do you guarantee I'll pass?

No, and nobody honest can promise that. Routerel helps you practise consistently, fix your mistakes and see, from your readiness score and the simulation, what's left to work on.

Is Routerel affiliated with Cisco?

No. Routerel is an independent app, not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by Cisco Systems, Inc. Cisco and CCNA are trademarks of Cisco Systems, Inc.

What data do you collect?

Only what's needed for your account and progress: your email, display name, answers, settings and subscription status. No ads, no tracking and no payment data (only Google Play sees your card). It's all explained in the privacy policy.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Account → Delete account. Or email us at contact@routerel.app. Note: deleting your account doesn't cancel your subscription; cancel that in Google Play. Full steps on the account deletion page.

I have an iPhone. Will it work?

For now, Routerel is Android only.
